Source of truth: `SECURITY.md` section 14.

## How to report

Report a suspected vulnerability privately, using GitHub private vulnerability reporting on the repository's Security tab. Do not open a public issue, pull request, or discussion for a vulnerability, and do not test against accounts or funds you do not own.

<Note>
  Assumption to confirm before the repository is made public: private vulnerability reporting is a repository setting that must be enabled by a maintainer. No dedicated security email address is published, and none is invented here. If the setting is not enabled when you read this, open a minimal public issue asking for a private contact, with no technical detail.
</Note>

## What to include

The affected component and file, the commit or deployment you tested, the steps to reproduce, the impact you believe it has, and whether any funds, real or testnet, were involved. A proof of concept against the Monad testnet deployment is welcome. Do not run one against mainnet systems you do not own.

## Scope

In scope: the contracts in `contracts/src`, the libraries and apps in this repository, and the testnet deployment at `app.monadeum.xyz` and `api.monadeum.xyz`. Of particular interest are the spend-policy and x402 signature paths, the reallocation contract's owner boundary, authorization in the API, and the handling of keys.

Out of scope: third-party services (Privy, ZeroDev, Kuru, Ramp, Nansen, DeFiLlama, Monad infrastructure) beyond how this project uses them, denial of service by volume, and social engineering of maintainers. A weakness in a third party that this project depends on is still worth telling us about, because the dependency is ours.

## What to expect

There is no bug bounty. This is testnet software and no program is promised or implied. Acknowledgement times and a fix timeline are not committed here, because no process has yet been run to measure them. Reports will be handled in good faith. No legal safe-harbor terms are published yet.

## Known limitations are already documented

Before reporting, check [Audit status](/security/audit-status), [Threat model](/security/threat-model), and `SECURITY.md` section 13. A limitation already listed there is a known and accepted state. A new way to exploit one, or an interaction that makes one worse, is exactly what is worth reporting.
