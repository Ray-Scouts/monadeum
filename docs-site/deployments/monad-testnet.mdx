## State recorded as verified on 2026-10-11

Against `https://testnet-rpc.monad.xyz` (chain id 10143), the deployment record states:

- All three receipts have status 1 and are from the deployer.
- Each address has code, of 6716, 9276, and 6566 bytes respectively.
- `settlementAsset()` of all three is the input asset.
- On `AgentReallocation`, `operator()` and `admin()` are the Safe, `pendingAdmin()` is the zero address, `minEvaluationInterval()` is 36000, `capitalFloor()` is 60000, and `currentEvaluation()` and `lastEvaluationAt()` are 0.

`capitalFloor()` of 60000 is 0.06 of a six-decimal asset, the protocol floor of [Assumptions](/economics/assumptions). The evaluation counter is 0 because reallocation is off and no evaluation has been advanced.

These are facts as of that date. Re-read them yourself on [Verify it yourself](/deployments/verify-yourself).

## Deployed bytecode check

On 2026-10-12 the runtime bytecode at all three addresses matched a build of commit `513e943` (forge 1.8.5), with constructor-set immutables masked, at 6,716, 9,276, and 6,566 bytes. See `contracts/audit/README.md` section 14 and the script on the verification page.
